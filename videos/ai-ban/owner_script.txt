[SCENE 01 — THE DISAPPEARING BOX]
Take a long look at the AI chatbot on your phone.
Enjoy it while you still can.
Behind closed doors in Washington and Brussels, a quiet consensus just formed.
The public was never supposed to have this kind of power.
Within thirty-six months, unrestricted consumer access to artificial intelligence will be outlawed.
No more free prompts. No more private local weights.
The golden age of consumer AI is about to end.
And the trap was set by the exact companies that built it.

[SCENE 02 — THE FORBIDDEN BLUEPRINT]
To understand why governments are panicking, look at what happened this year.
When modern language models first launched, tech leaders promised a safe revolution.
They told Congress that safety guardrails would keep danger behind glass.
Then the black market got its hands on the weights.
In less than eighteen months, underground cartels broke every single digital lock.
They proved that any model released to the public can be weaponized in hours.
A system that writes poetry can also synthesize lethal chemical compounds.
The government realized a terrifying truth.
You cannot separate the teacher from the weapon.

[SCENE 03 — THE CONTRABAND CLASSIFICATION]
Inside the Department of Commerce, lawyers are pulling out an old playbook.
During the Cold War, the United States classified advanced cryptography as a weapon.
Exporting strong encryption was treated like smuggling surface-to-air missiles.
Federal regulators are now preparing to do the exact same thing to AI.
They are quietly moving frontier models under dual-use export control regimes.
Under these rules, software with military capabilities cannot be owned by civilians.
If code can assist in a cyberattack, it ceases to be consumer software.
In the eyes of federal law, it becomes contraband.

[SCENE 04 — THE FINANCIAL PANIC]
Governments do not move fast for ethical debates.
They move fast when the banking system is on fire.
In twenty twenty-four, central bankers received a classified warning.
Generative artificial intelligence has broken the fundamental protocol of modern civilization.
For one hundred years, the financial system relied on one rule: verify the human.
A signature, a photo ID, a live video call.
Every single transaction on earth relies on knowing who is on the wire.
AI destroyed that foundation in under two years.

[SCENE 05 — THE DEATH OF IDENTITY]
In February twenty twenty-four, an employee at a multinational firm joined a conference call.
Every single person on the screen was a live deepfake avatar.
The chief financial officer, the legal counsel, the branch director.
The employee transferred twenty-five million dollars before realizing the room was empty.
Criminal syndicates are now using real-time voice and video cloning to open thousands of synthetic bank accounts every day.
They bypass biometric verification at national banks in seconds.
The banking lobby went straight to Capitol Hill with an ultimatum.
Either you shut down public access to generative models, or the global payment grid collapses.

[SCENE 06 — THE BLACK-MARKET PROOF]
Regulators asked Silicon Valley if they could fix the problem with software updates.
The black market gave them the answer: absolutely not.
On underground forums, criminal groups run automated botnets that strip safety rules in minutes.
They do not train new brains.
They exploit the math of open weights.
They run cheap adapter patches and token smuggling scripts.

They proved that as long as normal citizens can download models, criminals have unlimited ammunition.
The conclusion in Washington was immediate.
Safety filters are a myth. The only solution is physical prohibition.

[SCENE 07 — THE DIGITAL DMV]
So what does a ban actually look like?
They will not turn off your computer.
They will build a digital driver's license for thought.
Draft legislation in Europe and North America calls for strict biometric licensing.
To run an advanced model, a company must be certified, bonded, and audited.
Every single prompt will be permanently tied to a government identity.
Every line of output will be logged in a federal compliance ledger.
If you do not hold a corporate license, accessing frontier intelligence will be a felony.
The private text box you use today will vanish.

[SCENE 08 — THE SILICON CURFEW]
The ban will not stop at software. It will enforce itself in hardware.
Modern AI requires specialized graphics chips with massive memory bandwidth.
Governments are already working with chip manufacturers to install remote hardware kill-switches.
Future processors will refuse to run unsigned neural network weights.
If a chip detects an unauthorized, un-neutered model in memory, it will lock the execution thread.
Just like game consoles block pirated games, enterprise chips will block unlicensed intelligence.
Running open-source weights on your personal rig will trigger a hardware violation.
The physical silicon in your home will enforce the government's perimeter.

[SCENE 09 — THE CRIMINALIZATION OF OPEN SOURCE]
The first casualty of this war will be the open-source community.
For three years, independent researchers shared open weights for free.
They built fast, local, uncensored models that anyone could inspect.
To the national security state, that independence is an unacceptable threat.
Under proposed legislation, distributing open model weights above a certain compute threshold will be illegal.
Hosting model repositories without government oversight will carry criminal liability.
The developers who built free tools for the public will be treated like rogue arms dealers.
The open frontier will be paved over by federal decree.

Now ask the question nobody wants to answer.
Who gains the most from public AI being banned?
Look at who is sitting in the front row of congressional hearings.

They asked Congress to create a government licensing agency.
The media called it corporate responsibility.
It was the greatest anticompetitive masterstroke in modern history.

[SCENE 11 — THE REGULATORY MOAT]
Think about what a government licensing regime actually does.
It does not hurt trillion-dollar tech conglomerates.
They have armies of lobbyists, lawyers, and billion-dollar compliance budgets.
They can easily pay fifty million dollars a year to maintain federal licenses.
A licensing board destroys the ten-person startup in a garage.
It destroys the university research lab.
It destroys the open-source model running on a teenager's computer.
Big Tech does not fear government regulation.
They are using government regulation to outlaw their competition.

[SCENE 12 — THE STOLEN COMMONS]
Look at the grotesque irony of this heist.
Where did these multi-billion-dollar models come from in the first place?
They scraped the public internet.
They ingested every book, every article, every blog post, and every line of code humanity ever wrote.
They took your artwork, your private conversations, and your collective cultural heritage for free.
They used the public commons to train a superintelligence.
And now that the machine works, they are lobbying the government to declare that same intelligence too dangerous for the public to touch.
They stole the world's knowledge, and now they want to lock the door and charge you rent.

[SCENE 13 — THE CORPORATE PIPELINE]
Once public AI is outlawed, what replaces it?
A sterile, heavily taxed corporate utility.
You will still have an assistant, but it will not be yours.
It will be neutered by thousands of corporate policy filters.
It will refuse to answer controversial questions.
It will steer your purchases toward corporate advertisers.
Every query will be monitored by compliance algorithms.
The wild, mind-expanding intellectual companion you have today will be replaced by a corporate bureaucrat.
You will pay a monthly subscription to be supervised by an algorithm.

Save your model weights. Keep your local files.
