# surveillance-pricing · The Death of the Price Tag

## Why this topic
- **Never done before**: Zero overlap with existing or legacy films (captchas, spruce-pine, sam-altman, ai-ban, china-ai-underground, why-ai-dumber, elon-musk).
- **The "So What?" Test**: Maximum direct viewer stakes. Every single viewer shops online, books travel, orders delivery, and watches prices fluctuate mysteriously.
- **Shocking Cold Open**: Two phones side-by-side; one pays $65 more simply because their battery is at 6%.
- **Sourced Smoking Gun**: The July 2024 FTC Section 6(b) federal inquiry into "Surveillance Pricing" targeting 8 shadow giants (Mastercard, Revionics, Bloomreach, Task Software, McKinsey, PROS).
- **Historic Twist**: The 1861 invention of the price tag by John Wanamaker to guarantee equality, now assassinated by algorithms.
