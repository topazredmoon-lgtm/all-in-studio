## Facts on screen
- UC Irvine: Searles, Prapty & Tsudik, "Dazed & Confused: A Large-Scale Real-World User Study of reCAPTCHAv2", arXiv:2311.10911 (2023): 819 million hours. Years = our arithmetic (819,000,000 ÷ 8,766 ≈ 93,400).
- Yahoo 2000 sign-up abuse; Luis von Ahn a first-year PhD student at Carnegie Mellon: see nsdt captchas-p1/SOURCES.md.
- "CAPTCHA" coined 2003: von Ahn, Blum, Hopper, Langford, EUROCRYPT 2003, doi 10.1007/3-540-39200-9_18.
- ~200 million CAPTCHAs a day, ~10 s each; "So then I started feeling bad": Luis von Ahn, TED 2011 (quoted as text only). 63 years a day and 555,556 hours a day = our arithmetic (10 s × 200 M = 2 billion s).
- Two-word mechanism and agreement: von Ahn et al., Science 2008, doi 10.1126/science.1160379. "overlooks" / "inquiry" are illustrations.
- ~30 % of words missed by OCR on older books: von Ahn, TED 2011. The Dickens line is an illustration (public domain).
- 1.1 billion people typed a word from a book: Luis von Ahn, Big Think.
- Google acquired reCAPTCHA, 16 Sept 2009, "to improve our books and newspaper scanning process"; reCAPTCHA protected "more than 100,000 websites": Official Google Blog, "Teaching computers to read: Google acquires reCAPTCHA" (page capture `google_blog_2009_top.jpg`). Price undisclosed.
- reCAPTCHA as a Carnegie Mellon spin-off: widely reported (Wikipedia "reCAPTCHA"); add a primary citation before publishing.
- Duolingo founded 2011; 2013 BuzzFeed and CNN translation deals: Social Media Today (secondary; find a primary before publishing).
