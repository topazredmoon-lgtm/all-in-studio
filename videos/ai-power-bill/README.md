# THE PRICE OF THE PROMPT — 2-min sample

AI's electricity bill, paid on the viewer's own meter. Topic chosen 2026-10-14 (owner:
"new video topic, decide of everything then open first a 2min sample").

## Why this topic

- **Freshest possible stakes (2025-26):** EIA's Feb 2026 price jumps (+26.3% Virginia,
  +21.9% Ohio, +19.5% Pennsylvania YoY); PJM's record capacity auctions ($28.92 →
  $269.92 → $329.17/MW-day, then $333.44); DOE/LBNL's projection of 6.7-12% of US
  electricity by 2028; the July 2026 DOE 202(c) emergency orders that had data centers
  ready to switch to diesel within 15 minutes so homes kept air conditioning.
- **The viewer's own wallet:** the only subject in this studio's catalogue where the
  viewer's stake is literally a line on their utility bill.
- **A reversal:** the grid's emergency plan treats AI as a guest and the viewer's air
  conditioning as the necessity.
- **Never covered here:** no overlap with captchas / ai-ban / china-ai / why-ai-dumber /
  elon-musk / greyhat / stolen-voices / surveillance-pricing.
