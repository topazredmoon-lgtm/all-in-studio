## Wording guardrails for the sample

- "The price of power jumped" in Virginia/Ohio/Pennsylvania = EIA average revenue per kWh, YoY, Feb 2026 (P1). Not a single utility's residential tariff change.
- P4's "ten times more" refers to the headline RTO clearing price ($28.92 to $329.17), not any individual bill line.
- P5 is one state regulator's projection, said as such.
- P7 must keep "to be ready to" - the emergency curtailment was authorized and prepared, but never executed.
- No per-query energy figures are used (they are contested); the sample counts the machine in shares of national electricity instead.
