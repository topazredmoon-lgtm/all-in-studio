# Channel strategy · All-in studio

Niche: **money-safe AI explainers**. Every claim sourced, every clip CC / PD / government, no news clips.
Audience: curious adults, no jargon, "put yourself in the boots of the critical viewer."

## Positioning
- Hook-first, drama over neutrality, concrete 2025-26 events.
- Picture carries the idea; text supports. No lone big text.
- receipts in SOURCES.md / LICENCES.md → trust moat for growth and partnerships.

## Upload cadence (target once pipeline is steady)
- 1 long film per 2-3 weeks (8-13 min)
- 3 Shorts per long film (30-45 s from the strongest beats)
- 1 community post per upload (poll on next topic)

## Packaging rules
- Title <= 60 chars, number or stakes in first 40 chars
- Thumbnail: real frame + red 2-3 word text, no faces without context
- First 30 s = hook clip + open question; no calm setup
- Chapters from acts (already generated in publish/*.md)
- SRT from publish/*.srt; claim "CC-sourced visuals" in description

## Monetization path
1. Reach 1k subs / 4k watch hours: publish consistent (1 film + 3 Shorts / 2-3 weeks), end screens, cards.
2. Mid-roll eligibility at 8 min: keep full films >= 8 min.
3. Secondary revenue: Patreon "sources + receipts" tier, newsletter with SOURCES digests, sponsored "licence-safe" integrations only.
4. Avoid: fair-use bets, news clips, TED; protect revenue first (money rule).

## Next 3 ships
1. The Coming AI Ban (full, 12:54) — ship as-is, gather retention data.
2. China AI Underground (expand 1:18 sample to 8 min) — high search demand (DeepSeek, chip smuggling).

## KPIs to check weekly
- CTR thumbnail, avg view duration, % viewed, shorts-to-long conversion, subscriber conversion per video, top traffic source.
- If a video < 3% CTR: new thumbnail first, title second.
- If retention drops before 30 s: rewrite first 15 s only, re-upload as v2.
